Our story

Gold craft from Amritsar, since 1978

Since 1978, Sardar Jewellery House has specialised in one thing — plain, lightweight gold ornaments for women — now in 14K, 16K, 18K, 20K and 22K. From our workshop in Amritsar, we supply retail jewellers across Punjab and beyond.
