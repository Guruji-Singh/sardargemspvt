Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026
In short
  • We collect only what we need to verify your shop and serve your orders.
  • We never sell your details.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your details at any time on WhatsApp.

1. What we collect

  • When you register: your name, shop or company name, mobile number, city, GST number (if given), and a photo of your visiting card or ID.
  • Your password: stored in a protected form. We cannot see it.
  • Your design lists: the designs you select. They stay on your phone until you send them to us on WhatsApp.
  • Basic technical data: such as browser type and pages opened, used to keep the website working and safe.

2. Why we use it

  • To check that you are a genuine jeweller or trader before showing full stock and rates.
  • To reply to your enquiries and process your orders.
  • To send you new designs or updates, if you join our WhatsApp group. You can leave it any time.
  • To keep records required by law, such as GST.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your details. We share them only with:

  • Service providers that run this website for us: Cloudflare (website hosting), Supabase (accounts), Cloudinary (photos and documents) and Hostinger (stock system). They store data only to provide their service.
  • Courier or logistics partners, only the details needed to deliver your order.
  • Government authorities, if the law requires it.

4. Cookies and storage on your phone

The website saves small pieces of information on your phone or computer to keep you logged in and remember your design list. We do not use advertising cookies.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account details while your account is active. Billing records are kept for the time required by tax laws. If you close your account, we delete your registration details, except what we must keep by law.

6. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what details we hold about you,
  • correct or update them,
  • delete them, or withdraw your consent.

Send the request on WhatsApp to 98773 13252. We will reply within 7 working days.

7. Safety

Your details are sent over a secure (https) connection and stored with access limited to us. No system is fully secure, so please keep your password private.

8. Grievance contact

For any complaint about your data, contact Sardar Gems, Amritsar, Punjab 143001, on WhatsApp or phone at 98773 13252.

Questions?
