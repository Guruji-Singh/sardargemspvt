Shipping & Delivery

Last updated: 9 November 2026
In short
  • Ready stock is usually dispatched within 1–3 working days after confirmation and payment.
  • Delivery by our own staff, an insured courier, or pickup from our office in Amritsar.
  • Exact timing and charges are told to you before dispatch.

1. Where we deliver

We deliver to jewellers and traders across India. For orders from outside India, please contact us on WhatsApp.

2. How we deliver

  • Our staff: regular visits to many cities in Punjab and nearby states.
  • Insured courier: sealed, insured parcels through a trusted jewellery courier.
  • Pickup: collect from our office in Amritsar by appointment.

3. Dispatch time

  • Ready stock: usually 1–3 working days after the order is confirmed and payment terms are met.
  • Made-to-order: the time is told to you when the order is confirmed.
  • Delays can happen on holidays, during strikes or bad weather, or for other reasons outside our control. We will keep you informed.

4. Charges

Courier and insurance charges, if any, are told to you before dispatch and shown on the bill.

5. Receiving your parcel

Do not accept a parcel with a broken or tampered seal. Please record a video while opening the parcel and check the pieces against the bill. See Returns & Exchange for what to do if something is wrong.

6. Documents

Every dispatch comes with a GST invoice. Hallmarked pieces carry their HUID as required by law.
