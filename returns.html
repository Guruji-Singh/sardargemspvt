Returns & Exchange

Last updated: 9 November 2026
In short
  • Check every piece when you receive it.
  • Report any manufacturing defect within 7 days on WhatsApp, with photos or a video.
  • Defective pieces are repaired or exchanged. Change-of-mind returns are not accepted.

1. Check on delivery

Please check the parcel seal, the number of pieces and the weights against the bill when you receive the goods. If the seal is broken or something is missing, tell the delivery person and message us immediately. An opening video of the parcel helps us resolve it quickly.

2. Manufacturing defects

  • Report a defect (for example a broken joint, loose stone or faulty lock) within 7 days of delivery, on WhatsApp, with photos or a video and the bill number.
  • Send the piece back unused, with its tag. We check it and then repair it, replace it, or adjust it against your next order.
  • Damage from use, wear, wrong handling or alteration by someone else is not a manufacturing defect.

3. Weight or purity concerns

If you have any doubt about weight or purity, contact us within 7 days with the bill. We will re-check the piece together with you. Hallmarked purity can also be verified at any BIS-recognised centre.

4. What we do not take back

  • Pieces ordered and supplied correctly, returned only because of a change of mind.
  • Made-to-order pieces (from catalogues) made as per your order.
  • Pieces without their tag, or altered, resized or used.

5. Exchange of old stock

Exchange of unsold stock, if any, is decided case by case and agreed with you in advance. Rates and deductions are confirmed before the exchange.

6. Refunds

Where a refund is agreed, it is paid to your business bank account or adjusted in your account with us, usually within 7 working days of receiving and checking the goods.
